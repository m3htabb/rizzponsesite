Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Introduction

Rizzponse ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") operates the Rizzponse service (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our Service, including our AI reply suggestion feature.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an account or register for our Service
  • Make a purchase or subscribe to a plan
  • Contact us with inquiries or support requests
  • Participate in surveys or provide feedback
  • Submit any content or communications through the Service

This may include your name, email address, phone number, payment information, and any other details you choose to provide.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access the Service, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique identifiers)
  • Browser information (type, version, settings)
  • IP address and geolocation data
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used, duration of use)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

1.3 Screenshot and Conversation Data for AI Suggestions

When you choose to use the AI reply suggestion feature, you may upload or select a screenshot of a conversation. The app extracts conversation text from that screenshot so it can generate suggested replies. The extracted conversation text may include message content, display names, usernames, phone numbers, or other personal information that appears in the screenshot.

Rizzponse does not send the screenshot image itself to OpenAI for reply generation. Only the extracted conversation text needed to generate a response is sent after you give permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To extract conversation text from screenshots you submit and generate AI-powered reply suggestions
  • To process transactions and send related information
  • To send promotional communications (with your consent)
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To monitor and analyze Service usage and trends
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to third parties except as follows:

  • AI Processing: With your permission, we send extracted conversation text from your screenshot to our backend and to OpenAI, LLC so OpenAI can process the text and generate AI reply suggestions. We do not send the screenshot image itself to OpenAI for this purpose.
  • Service Providers: We may share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, app, payment processing, analytics, hosting, support, and other business operations
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction
  • Aggregated Data: We may share anonymized, aggregated data that cannot identify you

5. AI-Based Features and User Permission

Rizzponse offers AI-based reply suggestions. Before extracted conversation text from a screenshot is sent to a third-party AI service, the app asks for your permission. If you do not provide permission, the extracted conversation text will not be sent to OpenAI for AI reply generation.

What is sent: the extracted conversation text from the screenshot you submit, along with any settings needed to generate a useful reply, such as tone or spice level.

Who receives it: Rizzponse's backend and OpenAI, LLC.

What is not sent: the screenshot image itself is not sent to OpenAI for reply generation.

Why it is sent: the extracted text is used to understand the conversation context and generate suggested responses. We do not sell this conversation text.

We require service providers that process personal data for us, including AI service providers, to protect the data they process for us using privacy and security protections that are the same as or substantially equivalent to those described in this Privacy Policy.
